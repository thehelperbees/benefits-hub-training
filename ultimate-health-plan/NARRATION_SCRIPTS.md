# Ultimate Benefits Hub Training: Narration Scripts

Record each clip in ElevenLabs (same voice profile as the other partners), then run the usual two-pass loudnorm
(I=-20.2 LUFS, TP=-1.5 dBTP, LRA=11, 128 kbps / 44.1 kHz / mono MP3) and drop the file in that module's
`assets/audio/` folder using the exact filename shown. Module 1 (shared) needs no new audio.

Scripts are written to complement the on-screen text, not repeat it. No URLs are read aloud; where the screen shows one,
the script says "the sign-in page you bookmarked".

---

## Module 2: Accessing Benefits Hub and Signing In (`module-2-signing-in/assets/audio/`)

**01_cover.mp3**
Welcome. In this module, you'll get set up to sign in to Benefits Hub, the place where you'll look up Ultimate members and manage their Companion Care referrals. It only takes a few steps, and by the end you'll know exactly what to expect on your first login.

**02_step1_signin.mp3**
Your account team sends you an email and a password. Go to the Benefits Hub sign-in page, and bookmark it right away, because you'll come back to it every day. Enter your email and password, then select Sign In.

**03_step2_verify.mp3**
Right after you sign in, Benefits Hub sends a six-digit code to your email. Grab that code, type it in, and you're through. It's a quick extra step that keeps member information protected.

**04_tip_fact.mp3**
Here's something worth knowing. Those verification codes don't last forever. Each one expires ten minutes after it's sent. So, what if you open the email a little late?

**05_tip_reveal.mp3**
No problem at all. Just go back to the sign-in page and enter your email and password again. That sends you a brand-new code. You don't need to contact anyone for this one.

**06_step4_home.mp3**
And you're in. This is your home screen. The big search bar in the middle is where you'll start almost everything, looking up a member by their ID. We'll practice that in the next module.

**07_trylogin.mp3**
When your credentials arrive, try signing in for real. It's the best way to confirm everything is set up right. You don't have to do it now to keep going, but if you've already tried, let us know how it went.

**08_trylogin_yes.mp3**
Nice work, you're in. One thing to keep in your back pocket: if you ever forget your password, use the Forgot your password link on the sign-in page, and it'll walk you through a reset.

**09_trylogin_no.mp3**
That's okay, it happens. Reach out to your account manager and they'll help you get signed in. You can keep going with this training in the meantime, and try again later.

**10_outro.mp3**
That's everything you need to sign in with confidence. Once your credentials arrive, you can always come back here for a quick refresher. Next up, finding your member.

## Module 3: Finding and Reviewing a Member (`module-3-finding-your-member/assets/audio/`)

**01_cover.mp3**
Now that you're signed in, let's find a member. In this module, you'll search, explore a member's landing page, and learn to read their benefit tile.

**02_step1.mp3**
The fastest way to find a member is by their Member ID. For Ultimate, that's the letters U L followed by seven digits. Type it into the search bar and press Enter. Don't have the ID handy? There's another way. Tap Advanced Search to take a look.

**03_step1_advsearch.mp3**
Advanced Search lets you look someone up with their first name, last name, and date of birth. Fill in all three and select Search. It's a solid backup any time the ID doesn't turn anything up.

**04_step2.mp3**
Once you open a member, you land on their profile. Two areas matter most here. Tap each one to take a closer look.

**05_step2_overview.mp3**
The Overview holds the member's basics: name, address, date of birth, phone, and preferred language. It's a quick way to confirm you have the right person before you do anything else.

**06_step2_recent.mp3**
Recent Activity shows the latest authorization, referrals, and notes. If a member calls to follow up, glance here first to see what's changed.

**07_step3.mp3**
Scroll down and you'll find the Benefits section. Ultimate members have a single benefit tile, In-Home Support Services, Companionship. Select it, and you'll see the authorization dates and every referral under it. Use the enlarge button to look closer.

**08_practice_search.mp3**
Your turn. A member calls but doesn't have their ID. Use Advanced Search with their first name, last name, and date of birth to find them.

**09_practice_search_yes.mp3**
That's the one. Open their profile, and then tell us the member's ID, listed right under their name.

**10_practice_search_no.mp3**
No match yet. Check that the first name, last name, and date of birth are all entered exactly, then try again.

**11_practice_verify.mp3**
One last check. Look at the benefit tile and tell us when this benefit expires. Be careful to read the Expires date, not the Effective date.

**12_outro.mp3**
You can now find a member, read their landing page, and check their benefit. Keep the job aid handy if you need a refresher. Next, we'll create a referral.

## Module 4: Creating and Submitting a Referral (`module-4-adding-a-referral/assets/audio/`)

**01_cover.mp3**
In this module, you'll walk through creating a referral for Companion Care, from choosing the benefit all the way through checkout. Then you'll try it yourself.

**02_step1.mp3**
Every referral starts from the member's page. Start by selecting the benefit tile you want to create a referral for.

**03_step1_tile.mp3**
The tile opens the authorization details for this benefit. You'll see the dates it covers and any referrals already on file. When you're ready to start a new one, select Add Referral.

**04_step1_added.mp3**
Referral started. Select Next to move on and find the service.

**05_step2.mp3**
Now for the service. Begin with the category, then search for the service you need.

**06_step2_search.mp3**
For Ultimate, the category is In Home Support, and the service is Companion Care. Open the search box and pick it from the list.

**07_step2_selected.mp3**
Take a moment to check the service details, and make sure the status says Active.

**08_step2_altaddr.mp3**
One more thing to watch. If the member will receive care somewhere other than the address on file, like a relative's home, select change service address and enter the right location. It only applies to this referral. The address on their profile stays as is.

**09_step3.mp3**
Now set the quantity. Use the plus button to enter the number of hours the member needs, then select Add to Cart.

**10_step3_added.mp3**
It's in the cart. Select Next to review it.

**11_step4.mp3**
Almost done. Select the cart icon at the top of the screen to review what you've added.

**12_step4_mini.mp3**
Your cart shows the member's name. Select it to open the full summary.

**13_step4_summary.mp3**
Here's the summary. Check the quantity and total, and look closely at the service address. Once you check out, that address can't be edited. Fixing it means a support ticket and starting a new referral, so make sure it's right. Then select Checkout.

**14_step4_confirm.mp3**
One final confirmation. If everything looks right, select Confirm to submit the referral.

**15_step4_done.mp3**
The referral is submitted, and the cart is empty. Well done.

**16_practice_intro.mp3**
Now it's your turn, and this time there are no hints. Use what you just learned to submit a referral for Companion Care.

**17_outro.mp3**
You've selected a service, set the quantity, and checked out, all on your own. Keep this module handy anytime you need a refresher. Next, we'll look at managing a referral once it's in the system.

## Module 5: Understanding Benefits, Balances, Referrals, and Notes (`module-5-managing-a-referral/assets/audio/`)

**01_cover.mp3**
Once a referral exists, you'll spend a lot of your time reading it. In this module, we'll go through a referral piece by piece, so you know exactly what you're looking at and what to do about it.

**02_overview.mp3**
Here's a complete referral for Companion Care. It looks like a lot, but it's really just a handful of pieces. We'll take them one at a time.

**03_status.mp3**
Start with the status. It tells you where the referral stands right now. Select the Status field to see what each status means.

**04_status_reveal.mp3**
This table is your reference for every status. The one to watch is Accepted By Provider. That means the provider has confirmed, and it's your green light to share their contact information with the member.

**05_provider.mp3**
Next, the provider. Select the provider's name to open their contact card.

**06_provider_reveal.mp3**
You'll see the company, a contact name, email, phone number, and location. The phone number is visible no matter what, but only give it to the member once the referral is Accepted By Provider, which is the case here.

**07_narrative.mp3**
Now, the referral narrative. This is free-text space for context on the referral. Select it to see what's inside.

**08_narrative_reveal.mp3**
This one is empty, and that's perfectly normal. Still, make it a habit to check. Special instructions or background for the provider live here when someone has added them.

**09_addnote.mp3**
You can also leave a note on a referral. Open the Add menu and choose Add Note. Feel free to explore the View menu as well before you continue.

**10_addnote_open.mp3**
Pick a note type that fits, and write what happened in a sentence or two. General is a good default for everyday updates.

**11_addnote_submit.mp3**
Select Add Note to save it. Remember, a note is a record on the referral. It doesn't alert The Helper Bees. If something needs attention, submit a support ticket instead.

**12_addnote_viewnotes.mp3**
View Notes shows every note on the referral in one table, so you can see the full history at a glance.

**13_addnote_viewdocs.mp3**
View Documents shows any files a provider has attached, like an invoice or a receipt.

**14_addnote_viewsdq.mp3**
View SDQ shows the answers to any service detail questions entered when a referral was created. Ultimate's Companion Care doesn't use them, so it comes up empty here. You may see answers on other plans.

**15_encsummary.mp3**
Now, the balance. Above the encounter list, the Encounter Summary shows how many hours are allowed, how many are used, and how many remain on this referral. For Ultimate, the benefit is thirty hours a year. Below that, Key Dates tracks the referral's history, from when it was received to when it was accepted.

**16_encounters.mp3**
Last piece: encounters. An encounter is created each time a provider completes a visit. Select the encounter row to open it.

**17_encounters_reveal.mp3**
Here's what the provider recorded: the time in, the time out, and the total hours. You'll also see the service tasks they completed, like social engagement and light housekeeping. This is your confirmation that the visit actually happened.

**18_review.mp3**
Here's the whole referral again, with everything you just learned. Take another look, and then answer five quick questions below.

**19_outro.mp3**
You can now read a referral from top to bottom: status, provider, narrative, notes, hours, and encounters. The job aid summarizes it all. Next, what to do when something goes wrong.

## Module 6: Managing Common Exceptions and Escalations (`module-6-managing-exceptions/assets/audio/`)

**01_cover.mp3**
Sometimes things don't go as planned. A visit gets missed, a referral looks off, or a member can't use their benefit. This module shows you how to handle those moments the right way.

**02_troubleshoot.mp3**
Before you escalate anything, run a few checks. A lot of issues can be solved right there. Open each of the four cards to see what to look for.

**03_troubleshoot_verify.mp3**
First, verify and search. Confirm you have the right member, and check every referral on the benefit, not just the first one you see.

**04_troubleshoot_read.mp3**
Second, read before you escalate. The narrative and notes often already explain what's going on. Compare what was requested against what the authorization allows.

**05_troubleshoot_time.mp3**
Third, give it time. If an authorization was just created or updated, wait a full day before treating it as a system problem.

**06_troubleshoot_escalate.mp3**
If you've checked all of that and you're still stuck, it's time to escalate. Choose the issue type based on the action that's needed, not just the topic. That's what sends it to the right team. Give them the details they need: the member's name and ID, the service, what you see, what you need, and what you've already tried. A screenshot helps too.

**07_recognize.mp3**
So, what counts as something to escalate? A missed visit, a problem with a referral or authorization, a member who can't use their benefit, or a system issue. These all go through the same support ticket.

**08_zdaccess.mp3**
You can reach the ticket form from anywhere in Benefits Hub. Select the person icon in the top right, then choose Submit a ticket. There's no need to remember a separate address.

**09_branch.mp3**
That takes you to the Member Support Form. It's the one place to report all of those exceptions.

**10_support_formfill.mp3**
Now let's fill it out. Every field helps the right team act faster. If a member detail doesn't apply, enter N/A instead of leaving it blank. Choose your issue type carefully, and put as much detail as you can in Additional Comments. Type in whatever you like for this practice, then select Submit.

**11_support_confirm.mp3**
That's what a submitted ticket looks like. You'd get a confirmation with your ticket number.

**12_after_submit.mp3**
Here's the good news. Once you submit, you're done. The ticket routes to the right team automatically, based on the issue type you picked. No email or follow-up needed.

**13_tip_prompt.mp3**
Let's test it. A member calls to say their Companion Care visit didn't happen today. What's the right move?

**14_tip_reveal.mp3**
Submit a support ticket with the details and the right issue type. That's it. A missed visit, a referral issue, and a benefit access problem all work the same way.

**15_tryit.mp3**
Ready for the real thing? Use the button to open Ultimate's actual support form and explore it. Only submit a ticket when you have a real issue.

**16_outro.mp3**
You now know how to troubleshoot first, recognize what needs escalating, and submit a ticket that gets results. One more module to go: a full walkthrough, and a quiz to earn your certificate.
